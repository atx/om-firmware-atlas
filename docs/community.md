## The people behind the work

Each project is run by its own maintainers. Thank them, follow their guides, and send bugs and ideas to their issue trackers.

## Where each kind of change belongs

- **A bug or idea for a modification, emulator or tool** goes to that project's own issue tracker. Every project page links to it as "Ask or report issues".
- **What a project does, what was tested and where to find it** belongs in the Atlas.
- **Questions and chat** fit a [community venue](../views/community/index.md).

## Ground rules

- Be kind, and credit people's work.
- Keep findings scoped to the exact camera, firmware, project and build. Missing details stay visibly missing.
- Private messages or channels need permission before anything from them is published.
- Changes reach the site through a pull request. A maintainer reviews and merges; AI agents never approve or merge.
- Original contributions use the repository's [Apache-2.0 licence](https://github.com/xulfr/om-firmware-atlas/blob/main/LICENSE). Linked work keeps its own terms.

To prepare a change yourself, follow the [contribution steps](https://github.com/xulfr/om-firmware-atlas/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
