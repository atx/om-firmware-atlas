# 0002 — Community discovery is its own record type

Status: accepted, 17 November 2026.

The user requested active discussion venues, initially r/M43 and the Caduceus Discord invite published by the OM-3 project. Add `community` as a seventh record type and generate a prominent human directory from it.

A venue record identifies scope, listing basis, source, link-check state and related projects. Its existence does not substantiate firmware claims. Technical findings must cite a specific message/report and its publication permission. Broad photography discussion and project-specific support remain distinguishable.

An alternative was a hand-maintained list on the home page. Structured records make links reusable in project views, cheap agent routes and freshness checks without copying them. Do not add membership counts or claims of official endorsement that would require separate evidence and upkeep.
